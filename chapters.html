The Teaching

Part OneGod Hears What People Never HearHow our private words reveal the heart, shape our judgments of others, and test our integrity before God, even when no one else is watching. Part TwoThe Tongue Can Destroy What the Hands Never TouchHow careless, destructive, and repeated words can damage trust, injure relationships, and harm a person's reputation long before they know what has been said. Part ThreeWhen Gossip Dresses Itself in Spiritual LanguageHow spiritual language can disguise gossip, why spiritual gifts must be accompanied by godly character, and how prayer requests should be handled with truth, discretion, and love.

Bring the Teaching Together

ConclusionLet Your Words Reflect the Heart God Is ChangingFinish with a call to examine the heart, repair harm, and commit to truthful, responsible, and loving speech.

The curriculum and syllabus are designed for church groups, ministry teams, and personal study.
