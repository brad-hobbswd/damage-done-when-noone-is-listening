# The Damage Done When No One Is Listening

A Scripture-centered teaching website by Bradley Hobbs.

## Site files
- `index.html`: responsive landing page
- `index.css`: warm ivory, burgundy, gold, and sage-friendly styling
- `index.js`: responsive navigation, search toggle, and dynamic copyright year
- `banner.svg`: lightweight custom illustration of an open Bible and lantern
- `copyright.html`: copyright notice
- `terms.html`: terms of use
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy

## Design
The website uses a bright, welcoming palette with warm ivory and cream backgrounds, burgundy headings and buttons, gold details, and subtle botanical tones. The hero illustration is a separate SVG asset rather than a full-page background image.

## Navigation
The landing page and legal pages share the same site navigation and footer links. Navigation destinations for About, Chapters, Study Guides, Resources, and Contact are included, but those pages must be added before their links resolve.

## Before launch
The terms and privacy pages are general starting templates. Review them against the actual hosting configuration and third-party services before publication. Keep the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and banner files in the repository root so the relative asset paths work.
