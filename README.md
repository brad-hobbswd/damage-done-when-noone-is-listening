## About This Project

**The Damage Done When No One Is Listening** is a Christian teaching website about the relationship between the heart, our conversations, and our responsibility for the words we speak. It invites readers to let Scripture examine their motives, recognize the harm careless speech can cause, and pursue truthful, gracious, accountable communication.

The project is designed for **personal reflection, church teaching, and group study**. It emphasizes that spiritual growth involves more than knowing what is right. It means allowing the Word of God to shape how we speak and how we treat people, including those who are not present to defend themselves.

> “Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth...”  
> **Ephesians 4:29, KJV**

## A Necessary Distinction

This teaching challenges gossip, slander, and careless speech. It does **not** teach that people should conceal abuse, threats, exploitation, or serious misconduct. Responsible reporting, seeking appropriate help, and protecting people from harm are different from spreading stories for entertainment or personal advantage.

## Design and Technology

The website uses a warm, book-inspired visual identity:

- **Ivory and cream** backgrounds for a calm reading experience.
- **Burgundy** for headings and key elements.
- **Gold** accents for dividers and details.
- **Cormorant Garamond** and **Libre Baskerville** typography.
- Responsive layouts and shared navigation for desktop and mobile screens.
- JavaScript-powered navigation and site search.
