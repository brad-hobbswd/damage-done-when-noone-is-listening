A Question Worth Sitting With

Who are we becoming in the conversations we believe nobody will ever hear?

The absence of an audience does not remove our responsibility for what we say. This teaching invites us to examine the heart behind our words, distinguish gossip from responsible truth-telling, and let Scripture shape how we speak about one another.
